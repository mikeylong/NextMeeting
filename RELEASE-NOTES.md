# NextMeeting 1.0.0

NextMeeting shows your next meeting and a countdown in the macOS menu bar. Click it to see your meetings for the next 24 hours.

1. Choose from the calendars already connected to Apple Calendar.
2. See meeting details, attendees, and invitation responses.
3. Join Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex meetings.
4. Open the selected event in Apple Calendar, including the correct recurring occurrence.
5. Turn on Open at login if you want NextMeeting available when your Mac starts.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. NextMeeting requests calendar access, reads events locally, and never changes them or sends them to a server.

The download includes the app and a SHA-256 checksum. No personal calendar data or saved settings are included.

NextMeeting is signed with Developer ID and is not notarized. macOS may block it after download. [Apple’s opening instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) explain the approval available in Privacy & Security.
